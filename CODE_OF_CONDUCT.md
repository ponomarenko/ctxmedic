# Code of Conduct

ctxmedic follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination are not tolerated in issues,
pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@ponomarenko](https://github.com/ponomarenko). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block
contributors who break these rules.
